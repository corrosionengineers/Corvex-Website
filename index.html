Compressor components

View all compressor components
  • Piston ringsSeal the compression chamber between piston and cylinder liner, dry-running or lubricated.
  • Rider rings & wear bandsCarry the weight of the piston and keep metal off the cylinder liner.
  • Rod packing ringsSeal the piston rod where it leaves the cylinder, segment by segment.
  • Pressure breaker ringsAbsorb pressure pulses at the front of the packing so the sealing rings behind them last longer.
  • Oil wiper & scraper ringsKeep crankcase oil off the piston rod and out of the compression chamber.
  • Valve plates, discs & seatsPEEK and filled-PTFE sealing elements for suction and delivery valves.
  • Guide ringsGuide the piston and rod and keep running clearances even.
  • Bushes, liners & wear stripsSelf-lubricating bushes and liners for unloaders, gear sets and sliding parts.
  • Valve guides & unloader partsGuides, lifter pistons and unloader piston rings for compressor valves.
  • Replacement spares for leading makesCompatible PTFE and PEEK wear parts for the compressors already in your plant.
  • Compressor oilLubricating oils for reciprocating and screw compressors, supplied with your spares.

PTFE products

View all ptfe products
  • PTFE extruded rodsLong, continuous rods in virgin and filled PTFE, ready for machining.
  • PTFE moulded rodsLarge-diameter compression-moulded rods for heavy machined parts.
  • PTFE paste-extruded tubesThin-wall PTFE tubing for fluid lines, sleeving and insulation.
  • PTFE moulded tubes & bushesHeavy-wall tubes and bush blanks from small to very large diameters.
  • PTFE moulded sheetsThick PTFE sheets for gaskets, pads and machined plates.
  • PTFE skived sheets & filmThin sheets and film skived from moulded billets.
  • Modified PTFE (TFM-type)Second-generation PTFE with lower creep and a smoother, less porous surface.
  • Chemically etched (bondable) PTFEPTFE with an etched face that bonds to metal, wood, glass and plastics.
  • Bridge & structural bearing padsPlain and dimpled PTFE sliding pads for bridges and structures.
  • Anti-static PTFEConductive PTFE that dissipates static charge.
  • Machined components to drawingAny PTFE or PEEK part, machined from the right grade to your drawing.
