    Piston rings

    Seal the compression chamber between piston and cylinder liner, dry-running or lubricated.

    Duty
    Lubricated & oil-free
    Pressure
    Low to high-pressure stages
    Supply
    To sample or drawing

    Overview

    Piston rings hold the gas in the compression chamber. In a non-lubricated compressor they run dry against the liner, so the ring material decides how long the stage keeps its efficiency.

    Corvex rings are moulded and machined from filled PTFE and PEEK compounds chosen for the gas, the pressure differential and the liner material. For gas-specific and high-pressure duty we use our proprietary filler compositions to cut wear and extend service intervals.

    Types & designs

    • Single-piece rings: straight, angle (scarf) and step cut
    • Two- and three-piece rings
    • Half piston rings and compression rings
    • Segmental and pressure-balanced designs
    • Twin pressure rings

    Materials

    • Carbon-filled PTFE
    • Carbon + graphite PTFE
    • Bronze-filled PTFE
    • Glass-filled PTFE
    • Glass + MoS₂ PTFE
    • Graphite-filled PTFE
    • Proprietary Corvex compounds
    • Virgin & filled PEEK for high differential pressure

    Not sure which grade? Compare PTFE grades or ask an engineer.

    Overview

    In horizontal compressors the rider ring carries the piston. It must take the load, stay dimensionally stable when hot and wear slowly and evenly so the piston never touches the liner.

    We supply solid and cut rider rings, rider bands and rider-VLD piston assemblies in bronze-filled and carbon-filled PTFE and in PEEK for heavy pistons and high temperatures.

    Types & designs

    • Solid and cut rider rings
    • Rider bands / wear bands
    • Rider VLD piston assemblies
    • Wear rings

    Rod packing rings

    Seal the piston rod where it leaves the cylinder, segment by segment.

    Design
    Segmental, spring-loaded
    Parts
    Rings, cups, garter springs
    Duty
    Static & dynamic sealing
    Get a quote on WhatsAppAll compressor components

    Overview

    Rod packings stop gas escaping along the piston rod. Each packing cup holds a pair of segmental rings, a radial-cut sealing ring and a tangential-cut ring, held on the rod by a garter spring so they keep sealing as they wear.

    Corvex supplies complete packing sets and individual rings for every stage, including cylinder packings and suction packings.

    Types & designs

    • Radial- and tangential-cut ring pairs (3- and 6-piece)
    • Packing rings with or without garter springs
    • Packing cups and cylinder packings
    • Suction packing
    • Gland packing and seal rings for clearance

    Materials

    • Carbon-filled PTFE
    • Carbon + graphite PTFE
    • Bronze-filled PTFE
    • Glass-filled PTFE
    • Glass + MoS₂ PTFE
    • Graphite-filled PTFE
    • Proprietary Corvex compounds
    • Filled PEEK

    Pressure breaker rings

    Absorb pressure pulses at the front of the packing so the sealing rings behind them last longer.

    Position
    First rings in the packing case
    Materials
    Filled PTFE, PEEK
    Benefit
    Longer packing life
    Get a quote on WhatsAppAll compressor components

    Overview

    Pressure breaker rings sit at the cylinder end of the rod packing. They damp the pressure pulsation from each stroke so the rings behind them see a steadier load and wear more slowly.

    We make them in stiff, creep-resistant filled PTFE and PEEK, matched to the packing set they protect.

    Types & designs

    • Pressure breakers
    • L-type pressure rings
    • Pressure rings and twin pressure rings

    Materials

    • Carbon-filled PTFE
    • Bronze-filled PTFE
    • Virgin & filled PEEK

    Grades we offer

    Pure (virgin)

    Maximum chemical resistance and lowest friction. The all-round grade.

    Pure — electrical grade

    High-purity for insulators, bushes and electronic components.

    Carbon filled

    Higher compressive strength and hardness; good thermal conductivity.

    Glass filled

    Better wear and creep resistance with retained chemical resistance.

    Graphite filled

    Very low friction and wear; suits dry-running and soft mating surfaces.

    Bronze filled

    High wear resistance and heat dissipation for bearings and guide rings.

    MoS₂ + glass filled

    Harder, low-friction surface for dynamic seals and piston rings.

    Rulon-type (red)

    Bearing-grade compound for bushes, liners and guides.

    Turcite-type

    Low-friction slideway material for guide strips and wear bands.
