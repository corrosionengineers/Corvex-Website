    Oil wiper & scraper rings

    Keep crankcase oil off the piston rod and out of the compression chamber.

    Function
    Oil control
    Material
    Bronze & filled PTFE
    Design
    Segmental, spring-loaded
    Get a quote on WhatsAppAll compressor components
    3D · drag to rotate

    Overview

    Oil wiper and scraper rings sit on the crankcase side of the piston rod. They wipe the lubricating oil back into the crankcase so it cannot be carried into the gas.

    Corvex scraper rings are made in bronze and bronze-filled PTFE for a hard, sharp scraping edge, and supplied as complete segmental sets with springs.

    Types & designs

    • Oil wiper rings
    • Scraper rings
    • Segmental rings with garter springs

    Materials

    • Bronze
    • Bronze-filled PTFE
    • Carbon-filled PTFE
