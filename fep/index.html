    FEP rods, sheets & tubing

    Transparent, melt-processable fluoropolymer with PTFE-like chemical resistance up to +200 °C.

    Service
    −196 to +200 °C
    Forms
    Rods, Sheets, Skived sheets, Tubing
    Get a quote on WhatsAppAll other polymers
    3D · drag to rotate

    Overview

    FEP shares PTFE's chemical resistance, non-stick surface and electrical insulation, but it melts and flows, so it can be extruded, moulded, welded and heat-shrunk. It is also transparent, so you can see what flows inside FEP tubing or linings.

    Its upper service temperature is lower than PTFE (about +200 °C) and it is softer, so it suits linings, tubing, wire insulation and sight parts rather than heavily loaded components.

    Corvex supplies FEP rods, sheets, skived sheets and tubing.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is FEP the same as PTFE?

    Chemically similar, but FEP can be melted and welded and is transparent; PTFE handles higher temperatures.

    What is FEP's maximum temperature?

    About +200 °C continuous.
